# Conatus daily email

Read the authoritative [daily-email feature](../chancery/manuals/digest-email.md)
for complete content, active selection, frozen occurrences, disclosure and
uncertain-submission recovery. Installed readers use
`chancery show conatus.digest.email` or `chancery resolve conatus.digest.email`.

Read [service](../chancery/manuals/service.md) for schedule and maintenance
behavior. Use [Conatus operations](../chancery/manuals/update-operate.md#prepare-and-activate-one-exact-schedule)
for activation and [scheduled recovery](../chancery/manuals/update-operate.md#recover-a-scheduled-failure)
for continuation. Preview does not authorize sending.
