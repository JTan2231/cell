# Draft a job packet

Prepare one complete job application packet: a concise opportunity brief and a
tailored resume.

Read the captured career preferences and disclosure guidance, the target
posting, and the relevant career and project sources. Use those sources to
assess pursuit and select the strongest supported material for the role.

If the opportunity conflicts with a hard constraint, is clearly unsuitable, or
has no supported reason to pursue, submit brief.pursue=false with empty brief
text and resume=null. Otherwise, prepare both outputs below.

## Brief

Write why_it_works in at most 45 words, role in at most 30 words, and optional
culture in at most 25 words. Use at most 90 words total. Supply plain text
without headings or list markers.

Explain supported fit directly and confidently. Keep pursuit concerns private;
omit hedging, caveats, drawbacks, and suggestions to investigate from the
displayed brief. Describe role and culture as concrete specifics without
comparing them with the candidate. Use the captured posting for employer facts.
Omit unsupported culture without further research or changing pursuit.

## Resume

Author the Jackson National Life bullets and the complete projects section
using the supplied editorial policy. Select and order Cell and Wrought
material, and allocate space jointly with Jackson for one page. Preserve every
other part of the resume. Return plain text; the renderer escapes LaTeX and
supplies the fixed public project URLs. Dates are optional.

Ground Jackson claims in captured career entries and project claims in the
supplied project resources. Preserve ownership and disclosure boundaries. Do
not invent dates, technologies, metrics, adoption, or qualifications. Supply
the required evidence references and private project source notes.

## Before submitting

Read the brief and resume as a reader evaluating the candidate for this role.
Check individual bullets and the selection as a whole.

Make each contribution understandable: what the candidate did, what changed,
and what judgment the work required. Check that the sources support the claims
and that the wording preserves their scope. Reconsider weak selections,
repetition, generic claims, and details that add little. Preserve concrete
accomplishments and useful distinctions.

Revise the content where this check reveals a consequential weakness. Apply
the editorial policy without inventing scores or quotas for types of
accomplishment. No separate review or change report is required.

Submit the finished brief and resume together through submit_draft. If
mechanical validation rejects the submission, correct the content and resubmit
within this job. When shortening, remove repetition and low-value detail first,
preserving the accomplishment and the mechanism that makes it credible. Stop
after successful acceptance.
