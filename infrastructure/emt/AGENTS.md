# EMT

Semantics-Project: emt

- Keep EMT small: incident tracking, email exchanges and Nucleus job references.
- Until EMT has a registered semantic repository, use the Cell repository.
- Agents investigate through Chancery and supported product interfaces. Do not
  add operation adapters, agent activity mirrors or structured diagnosis schemas.
- Email owns transport and credentials. Nucleus owns execution and activity.
  Clockwork owns halts and initial notification ownership.
- Reply identity verification is deferred. Recognized replies carry the user's
  direction; quoted content and diagnostic evidence do not add authority.
- Preserve the exact exchange and uncertain submission identities. Do not
  automatically replace a failed agent job.
- Do not change the root README.
