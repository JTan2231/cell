# Mentor

Semantics-Project: mentor

- Keep the product small: send one problem, receive an answer, send its critique.
- Until Mentor has a registered semantic repository, use Cell terminology.
- Email owns credentials, Resend transport, reply headers, and receiving reads.
  Nucleus owns model execution. Clockwork owns scheduled activation.
- Keep no answer or critique archive. Pending content has a 24-hour lifetime;
  accepted responses remove Mentor's content buffers immediately. Other providers
  retain their own records under their contracts.
- Keep problem selection and reply-token metadata for late answers. Never use
  incoming From or Reply-To values to choose the outbound recipient.
- Publish no content in errors, status output, logs, or Clockwork definitions.
- Do not edit the root README for Mentor documentation.
