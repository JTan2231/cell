# EMT

EMT follows Clockwork incidents through personal email. It asks an agent to
investigate an eligible halt, retains the correspondence and Nucleus job
reference, and handles each recognized user reply as one intervention. It also
sends shared quota-condition notices without starting an agent.

EMT owns incident tracking, correspondence, frozen outgoing mail, worker
admission and its installed state. Clockwork owns halts, service-check policy,
notification claims and scheduling. Nucleus owns execution, activity and quota.
Email owns transport and credentials. Affected products own recovery and the
evidence that an intervention succeeded.

Read one feature with `chancery show ID`. Read a procedure and its required
feature contracts with `chancery resolve ID`. Read this installed overview with
`chancery product emt`. These queries do not establish readiness, execute work
or grant account, email, intervention or installation authority.

## Operations

Use `emt.installation.operate` to prepare, activate, inspect, back up or recover
an installation. It keeps prerequisites, effects, stop conditions and verification
with the procedure and requires the feature contracts for detailed explanations.

Use `emt.development.change` to change EMT or its prompt selection while
preserving public meaning, authority, exact identities, recovery and validation.

## How the features work together

Clockwork records the scheduling halt and checks whether it is alert-eligible.
EMT retains its incident, prepares an exact agent request and keeps the Nucleus
reference. The agent uses product-owned interfaces and freezes its report before
Email submission. Clockwork serializes initial-alert ownership with its basic
sender. Subsequent recognized replies create separate one-off assignments.

Quota and operator intent can defer discovery or admission. Frozen deliveries
and existing exchange recovery keep their own identities. Runtime completion
and Email acceptance are separate observations; the affected product establishes
whether the intervention succeeded. Sender verification is deferred, and an older
reply does not approve a newer halt.

Required documentation dependencies participate in compatibility and complete
resolution. Related entry references only provide navigation. Neither form
transfers product authority. No model accuracy, inbox completeness, timer latency
or final-delivery guarantee is promised. Use `nucleus manual` for shared Cell
maintenance and cross-product recovery order.
